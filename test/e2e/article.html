On reading one word at a time

The eye does not glide smoothly across a line of text. It jumps, fixates, and jumps again, and a surprising share of reading time is spent moving rather than recognising. Rapid serial visual presentation removes the jumps by bringing each word to a fixed point instead.

What Spritz added was the observation that within a word there is one position the eye prefers to land on, slightly left of centre. Align every word on that position and the eye can stop hunting for it.
